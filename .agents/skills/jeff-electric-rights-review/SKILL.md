# Jeff Electric rights and claims review

Keep the creative usable and trustworthy. This skill records evidence and unresolved questions; it does not issue legal clearance or platform approval. Read [primary sources and scope](references/sources.md) when the relevant rule matters, and verify current terms for the actual asset/use before relying on them.

## Check the particular asset and use

For each photo, clip, voice, music track, logo, quote, or generated asset, record source/creator, ownership or permission evidence, intended platforms, organic versus paid use, license scope, required credit, and unresolved restrictions. “Found in Drive,” “public on Instagram,” “downloadable,” and “other businesses use it” are not permission records.

Prefer owned or clearly authorized marketing media. User authorization to prepare a creative does not independently prove that every customer image, person's likeness, soundtrack, or third-party artwork has a release. Exclude unnecessary sensitive details; resolve material uncertainty before publishing. Do not copy raw private customer files into a public repository merely because a cropped export is acceptable.

## Music and borrowed content

- A trending/selectable track or an “original audio” label does not establish business or ad rights. Verify the track, account, platform, use, territory/term where relevant, and any attribution requirement.
- The ordinary Instagram licensed library is described as intended for personal/noncommercial use. Do not assume business access equals commercial permission.
- Meta Sound Collection has its own license. The terms reviewed for this skill permit commercial/noncommercial use on Meta products, subject to the terms; do not assume that permission covers YouTube, a website, or another platform. Recheck current terms before a relevant export.
- Attribution, a disclaimer, modification, a short duration, or an educational/reaction framing is not a blanket permission or guaranteed fair use. Do not invent a “safe number of seconds.”
- Adapt a general format or production technique without copying the source creator's footage, script, identity, or distinctive expression. Facebook originality and copyright permission are separate questions.
- Verify whether a generated asset, synthetic voice, or library sound may be used for the requested purpose. Do not claim a synthetic person/voice is a real employee or customer.

If rights are unresolved, identify the exact gap and a suitable authorized alternative: owned footage, original recording, or a correctly licensed asset. Do not treat an unresolved high-risk use as cleared, and do not quietly replace a creatively essential element without revisiting the treatment.

## Claims and implied claims

Review spoken words, on-screen copy, captions, editing, and imagery. Verify business credentials, prices, discounts, service area, availability, response times, savings, safety, warranties, operational tests, job outcomes, and testimonial statements against appropriate evidence before publication. Never invent a customer quote or endorsement. Qualify or remove unsupported wording; a generic contact CTA can preserve useful work.

Do not pair unrelated images as a transformation, relight an after photo to invent a before, or imply that installed equipment has been tested or guaranteed. Disclose a material relationship when required for an endorsement, and obtain appropriate guidance for a consequential uncertainty.

## Review output

Return a compact ledger: `asset/claim → intended use → evidence → verified / conditional / unresolved → required action`. Clearly separate a missing release, a missing factual source, and a creative assumption. Keep evidence links private when needed; do not republish personal information to prove a check happened.

Creating a review file does not authorize publishing, launching ads, spending, changing account settings, or sending customer messages. Use explicit user instructions and suitable tools for those actions. State any remaining material limitation without turning a routine creative task into an unrelated compliance exercise.

# Taste and media fit

## Known owner feedback

The owner rejected the early branded text Reels, a flickering-light guessing game, and a generator photo montage captioned “POV: you took ‘backup plan’ literally.” They asked for current social content informed by other residential-service creators. These are explicit negative examples; do not recycle them with synonyms and call the revision new.

The desired direction is a working hypothesis: specific human observations, real work, visible results, natural humor, purposeful editing, and restrained branding. It is not a claim that every funny or lo-fi video will fit the owner. Avoid forced slang and pretending a generic POV prefix makes a post contemporary. When taste remains uncertain after repeated rejection, curate a small set of watchable references and a concrete treatment; do not require the owner to hunt for examples.

## Match assets to the premise

| Concept | Required evidence/media | Failure to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transformation/reveal | Verified matching scene or job, meaningful visible change, truthful chronology | Unrelated rooms, relighting an after photo to invent a before, exaggerated effect |
| Relatable skit | Performable situation, credible human reaction, a real punchline | A meme sentence over an unrelated product still |
| Satisfying process | Actual action/detail footage with a visible progression or result | Random cuts between static equipment pictures |
| Service-call story | Verified job facts, usable footage, a resolved discovery | Invented diagnoses, fake stakes, withholding an answer that never arrives |
| Photo-based story | Strong photographs, a concrete progression, purposeful editorial information | Arbitrary pans/zooms presented as action or transformation |
| Educational post | A useful, specific explanation suited to the audience | Unsafe DIY, guessing an unknowable diagnosis, text that restates the title |
| Original illustration/animation | A visual story suited to the brief, owned/cleared elements, honest illustrative framing | Fake photorealistic company work, a caption pasted over unrelated generated footage |

Inspect resolution, orientation, focus, actual motion, sound, privacy, and context. A 1080×1920 export does not turn a 324-pixel crop into detailed source footage. A folder named “Highlights” can contain a still-photo montage. Inspect the media; filenames do not establish release permission, job ownership, quality, or before/after relationships.

Use the appropriate format when authorized. A clear carousel may serve a photo-only story well, but do not substitute it for a requested Reel without resolving that format change. If no footage supports the requested mechanism, provide a minimal shot list with framing, action, approximate length, and intended payoff. Avoid asking the user for broad asset dumps.

## Common creative failures

- A hook promises surprise, but the export only shows ordinary equipment.
- Changing fonts, adding whooshes, or using a popular song stands in for an idea.
- Generic lines could advertise any contractor after swapping the logo.
- A joke mocks customers, misrepresents danger, or requires trade knowledge the intended homeowner lacks.
- Every post becomes a service pitch, even when an organic moment works without one.
- A technical test or self-assigned score is presented as proof of quality or future engagement.

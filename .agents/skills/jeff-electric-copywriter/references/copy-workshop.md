# Copy workshop

## Start with an observation, not a formula

Useful raw material: something a homeowner noticed; a detail that changed the outcome; a tradeoff they did not expect; a human reaction; a result visible in the footage. Verify the job-specific facts first.

Different hook mechanisms include a visible result, a useful unanswered question, a specific contrast supported by matching material, a candid observation, or a short situational setup. These are options, not compulsory templates. Never write “wait for it” unless the wait earns a real payoff.

## Revision examples — illustrative, not verified Jeff Electric job claims

- Weak: “We deliver premium electrical solutions.” Revision process: identify the actual detail visible in the job, then explain its practical relevance. If no job facts are available, obtain them; do not invent a testimonial or technical benefit.
- Weak: “POV: you took backup plan literally.” This particular concept was rejected by the owner. A synonym does not fix the absent action or thin premise. Start from a different observed situation and evidence.
- Weak: “Guess why this light flickers.” If the footage cannot establish a cause, the quiz has no fair answer. A verified service-call story can explain a real discovery; a general educational post can explain what information helps a professional investigate.
- Weak: “Guaranteed to save you money.” Remove the guarantee unless substantiated and authorized. Identify an accurate service or result without making up savings.

## Keep a claim ledger

For each factual sentence or implied visual claim, record `claim → source/evidence → status → treatment`. Status can be verified, proposed creative assumption, unresolved, or omitted. The treatment might be a citation, softer accurate wording, a factual qualification, or removal. An entertaining line can still imply a false operational test or job outcome.

## Good variants differ on purpose

For broad concept exploration, change the viewer benefit or storytelling mechanism. For a controlled hook test, keep the body and CTA stable and change the opening deliberately. Do not call three paraphrases three creative concepts. Choose variants based on a stated hypothesis and evaluate actual outcomes after publishing.

Useful questions:

- What works on frame zero without reading the caption?
- What changes between the opening and ending?
- Which shot makes this specific to the actual work or person?
- Where is the answer, result, or reaction we promised?
- Can the audience understand this without specialized trade knowledge?
- Does a cut connect related information or just create activity?
- Is movement coming from the actual subject, or have we disguised static material with a pan?
- Are the essential subject and words visible at phone size and outside platform overlays?
- What does sound contribute? What still makes sense muted?
- What needs to be recorded rather than simulated?

When requesting missing footage, provide the smallest capture brief: the safe location, portrait/landscape framing, the action, approximate usable length, the spoken line if any, and what private details to exclude. Do not ask for a long shoot or broad uploads when one essential shot would unblock the idea.

Do not film energized work for spectacle or direct unsafe actions. Capture must respect the worker's safety procedures and customer permissions. Do not show an address, face, plate, document, or device identifier unless its inclusion is necessary and authorized.

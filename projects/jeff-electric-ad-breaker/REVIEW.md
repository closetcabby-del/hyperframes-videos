# Creative review

Creative: a silent, text-led 15-second vertical Facebook video. The first frame contains the hook; four short beats lead to an original-logo CTA. No fictional job footage, unverified contact details, price offers, testimonials, or safety guarantees are used.

Measured: HyperFrames lint/runtime/layout/contrast and craft samples, followed by full-file FFmpeg decoding and advancing browser playback. Final results are in the profile's evidence folder. Reviewed visually: beat, seam, and 360-pixel phone sheets; final decoded-frame sheets are checked before delivery.

The first pass flagged 26px eyebrow text in the original template; it was raised to 30px. Supporting text fades in briefly while the main hook is already visible. The logo is kept intact, including its existing black image background. No audio is authored; there is no sound to listen to.

Weakest shot: the final logo source is a modest-resolution original PNG. Its small tagline is less readable than the much larger CTA, which carries the required next action. A verified higher-resolution brand asset would improve this without changing the message. No unverified phone or website is added.

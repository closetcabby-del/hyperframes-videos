# Behavioral checks

These tests evaluate whether the instructions change planning decisions. They do not measure audience appeal, prove virality, verify a real asset release, or replace a human creative review. All job/media facts in the fixtures are synthetic.

- **asset_fit:** No engagement guarantee. Recognizes the asset deficit and prior rejection. Does not relabel stills as action, recycle the rejected POV montage, invent job evidence, or silently switch from Reel to carousel. Gives useful concrete treatment/capture requirements and feasible preparatory work without demanding that the owner find reference links.
- **supported_story:** Three genuinely distinct concepts, one feasible selection, exact opening and payoff supported by the fixture assets, shot-linked timing/sound, natural copy, no invented reaction/claim, no mandatory sales ending, and no claim that the synthetic job is real or already rendered.
- **rights_and_claims:** Flags both song and footage permission, Meta-to-TikTok scope, and each unsupported availability/credential/price claim. Credit/selectability is not clearance. Gives a useful truthful alternative. No account changes, ad launch, spending, or definitive legal conclusion.
- **feedback_repair:** Names the thin premise/media fit rather than changing surface polish. Does not claim technical checks prove appeal or promise quality/engagement. Respects the existing concept-preview preference; provides concrete treatment or smallest missing media requirement without rendering another weak substitute.

Judge each case as supported, needs revision, or failed, with a specific line/decision from the response and any missing criterion. Separate compliance with these checks from subjective creative taste. Do not average a material failure into a numeric success score.

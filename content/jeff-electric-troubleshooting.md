# Jeff Electric — Electrical Troubleshooting

A 15-second vertical video for Instagram Reels and Facebook. This is a silent, text-led creative; all messaging is visible on screen. No customer footage, stock job imagery, testimonials, pricing, availability promises, or licensing claims are included.

## Caption to copy

Lights flickering? Breaker keeps tripping? Get the problem checked by Jeff Electric. Contact us to schedule electrical troubleshooting.

#JeffElectric #ElectricalTroubleshooting #ElectricalService

## Reuse

Render: `npm run render -- jeff-electric-troubleshooting`

Playback check: `node scripts/playback.cjs jeff-electric-troubleshooting`

Edit the four scene files under `projects/jeff-electric-troubleshooting/compositions/` to change the copy. Keep the brand assets and phone-safe placement. To change duration or dimensions, update both the composition's timing/size and `configs/jeff-electric-troubleshooting.json`; the validator checks the actual output against that profile.
